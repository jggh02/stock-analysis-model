# HRP as default portfolio optimiser over Mean-Variance

The reallocation optimizer supports both Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) and classical Mean-Variance Optimisation (MVO) behind a strategy enum, with HRP as the default. We considered defaulting to Markowitz MVO (the textbook approach), but MVO requires inverting the covariance matrix, which is numerically unstable when holdings are highly correlated — common with Indian banking and IT sector clusters. HRP uses hierarchical clustering on the correlation matrix and allocates along the dendrogram, avoiding inversion entirely. It produces more stable, diversified weights out of the box with small-to-medium portfolios (5–30 holdings), which is our expected use case. MVO remains available for users who want to supply their own expected-return estimates and prefer the classic efficient-frontier formulation.
